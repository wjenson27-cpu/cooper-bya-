How to play

  • Move Cooper with the stick, or tap the yard where he should go.
  • Press DIG to dig dirt, sniff a bush, or nudge a toy.
  • Find all 3 orange and blue balls, then lead him to the nap spot.
  • Chase squirrels before they run up a tree for an extra star.
  • Sprinklers give a silly shove. Mud and tide pools only slow him down. Gnomes do not budge.
